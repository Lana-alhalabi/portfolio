About Me

I'm a student of Information Engineering specializing in Software Engineering, on a journey where logic meets aesthetics.

My work sits at the intersection of engineering precision and visual storytelling. I love building things that feel as good as they function — from a perfectly structured codebase to a meticulously crafted interface.

My personality is calm and curious, with a streak of adventure and a quietly mysterious edge. I'm drawn to detail: the luxurious ornament, the perfectly chosen typeface, the line of code that just clicks. Whether designing a brand identity or building a responsive layout, I bring the same care to every pixel.

I believe technology and creativity aren't opposites — they're the same impulse, expressed in different languages.
